Company Overview

Who We Are

Tianjin Lianhe Youxiang Technology Co., Ltd. is a professional cosmetic trading and manufacturing company based in Tianjin, China. Founded in 2019, we operate in strategic partnership with Tianjin Orient Natural Cosmetic — a Korean-invested cosmetic manufacturer with over 31 years of industry experience.

Our mission is to bring premium Korean-quality cosmetic manufacturing to global brands at competitive prices. We specialize in skincare serums, freeze-dried powder treatments, men's skincare, and color cosmetics — all available for private label, OEM and ODM customization.

With a factory area of 10,000–30,000 square meters and an annual output value of US$50–100 million, we serve B2B buyers across Eastern Asia and worldwide. We attend 10+ international exhibitions each year to connect with partners and showcase our latest innovations.

Our Factory

Advanced Manufacturing Facility

Our 10,000–30,000 sqm production facility features GMPC-standard clean workshops, fully automated filling and packaging lines, and a comprehensive quality control system. The factory is designed to handle both small-batch custom orders and large-scale production runs efficiently.

We maintain a large inventory of raw materials to ensure stable supply and short lead times. Our production team includes experienced technicians, many trained under Korean manufacturing standards.

  • GMPC & ISO22716 certified clean workshops
  • Automated emulsifying, filling and packaging lines
  • In-house microbiological and stability testing lab
  • 10,000–30,000 sqm production area
R&D Center

Innovation & Research

Our R&D center is staffed with a team of 10+ chemists and formulation scientists, combining Korean cosmetic technology expertise with continuous research into global skincare trends. We develop over 100 new formulations each year across skincare and color cosmetic categories.

The laboratory is equipped with advanced testing instruments for stability testing, microbial analysis, efficacy evaluation, and sensory assessment. Every new formula undergoes rigorous testing before being approved for production.
