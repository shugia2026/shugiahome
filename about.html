Company Overview

Who We Are

Tianjin Orient Natural Cosmetics Co., Ltd., founded in 1995, is a South Korean wholly-owned beauty enterprise located in Wuqing Development Zone, Tianjin. With over 30 years of development, we have built a complete industrial chain integrating R&D, design, production and sales, emerging as a powerful modern cosmetics manufacturer.

Our factory covers an area of 13,000 square meter and is equipped with high-standard Class 100,000 dust-free purification workshops. Manufacturing follows international standards, and we have obtained dual certifications of GMPC and ISO22716 for cosmetics. Strict full-process control ensures stable and safe products.

Supported by advanced South Korean beauty research resources, we have assembled a professional Korean R&D team focusing on gold skincare. Our core formulas have been upgraded to the 6th generation, and four major gold skincare series have been launched to meet moisturizing, nourishing, repairing, anti-aging and other skincare demands for consumers of all ages and skin types.

Our proprietary brand SHUGIA adheres to the philosophy of "Natural & Scientific Skincare". We reduce unnecessary additives and focus on developing safe, effective and cost-effective skincare products suitable for Asian skin, winning wide recognition in the market.

Besides operating our own brand, we provide professional OEM & ODM services for skincare products. We deliver one-stop full-chain solutions including formula development, product customization, package design, mass production and brand empowerment, flexibly catering to clients’ requirements for personalized customization and bulk orders.

Going forward, Oli Natural will keep focusing on the gold skincare track, persist in technological innovation and strict quality control, and cooperate with global partners for mutual growth with solid expertise.

Our Factory

Advanced Manufacturing Facility

Our 30,000 sqm production facility features GMPC-standard clean workshops, fully automated filling and packaging lines, and a comprehensive quality control system. The factory is designed to handle both small-batch custom orders and large-scale production runs efficiently.

We maintain a large inventory of raw materials to ensure stable supply and short lead times. Our production team includes experienced technicians, many trained under Korean manufacturing standards.

  • GMPC & ISO22716 certified clean workshops
  • Automated emulsifying, filling and packaging lines
  • In-house microbiological and stability testing lab
  • We exercise strict quality control over the whole production workflow
R&D Center

Innovation & Research

Our R&D center is staffed with a team of 10+ chemists and formulation scientists, combining Korean cosmetic technology expertise with continuous research into global skincare trends. We develop over 100 new formulations each year across skincare and color cosmetic categories.

The laboratory is equipped with advanced testing instruments for stability testing, microbial analysis, efficacy evaluation, and sensory assessment. Every new formula undergoes rigorous testing before being approved for production.
